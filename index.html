The Board — dispatched work in coordinate form

Projection: each task coordinate's three triples are packed base-100 (each component is < 100, so packing preserves order) into three scalars — U = Library.Shelf.Series, V = Collection.Volume.Book, W = Chapter.Section.Scroll. The three panels plot U–V, U–W, and V–W, so all nine dimensions are visible as pairwise shape. Point color is status; a ring marks tasks issued on the most recent day in the feed. This page re-fetches feed.json on load and every 60 seconds, so a rebuilt feed shows up without a page rebuild; the copy embedded at build time is the fallback.

Growth — dispatched tasks over time

Every dispatch in the feed, arriving in issued order on the board's U × V plane. Time is warped like a black hole — dilated at the first days and the last days, plunging through the middle (warp(x) = x + 0.85·sin(2πx)/2π), about 3 seconds end to end. The smooth green curve is the true cumulative count, drawn point by point (no stairsteps); the dashed curve is the exponential-decay model fitted to this build's register: N(t) = 382 − 353·e^(−0.406t), R² = 0.998. The daily dispatch rate, from its peak day, decays at λ = 0.41/day — a half-life of about 1.7 days.
